Why Parallax

A GROUP IS NOT
A SINGLE STYLE

The name is a method. When four people look at the same screen from different positions, the interface should show the shift — not flatten it.

01

Angle

We do not copy each other into one visual voice. Each player brings a different way of seeing; the layout has to hold the difference without losing the frame.

02

Interface

The screen is the meeting point: a portfolio, a case study, a mailbox, a character select. Everything should work, not just look like a game.

03

Evidence

We show the work and the process. Roles, tools, credits, and project iterations are part of the design — not hidden in a footnote.

How we work

FOUR MOVES,
REPEAT.

The process is deliberately simple. It works for a single page, a case study, or a final presentation.

01

Research

Collect references, ask questions, define the problem. No moodboard without a claim.

02

Sketch

Low-fidelity layouts and content models. Decide what the page is for before making it pretty.

03

Build

HTML, CSS, JS, images, motion. Keep it fast, accessible, and editable by the group.

04

Review

Test at real sizes, fix the copy, credit the work, present the thing out loud.

02 / Medium & linework

THREE TOOLS.
ONE SUBJECT.

Working on the same character across three mediums highlights how each tool shapes mood and contrast. Ink invites tight cross-hatching and architectural atmosphere; graphite slows down for smooth value transitions and symmetry; marker commits immediately to saturated color and graphic silhouette.

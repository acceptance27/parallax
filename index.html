QWEEPS / 2026

QWEEPS

Live interactive visual teaching

QWEEPS turns a question or a source document into a live, interactive visual lesson. It plans connected reasoning beats, generates SVG and ManimGL scenes, reviews rendered frames, and plays back with synchronized narration. Learners can pause, inspect objects, ask questions, and branch into a focused mini-lesson.
